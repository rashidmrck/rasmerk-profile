# Export PDF reports

Rasmerk can generate a monthly financial report as a PDF file.

## Requirements

PDF Reports is a **Premium** feature.

## Steps

1. Go to **Reports**.
2. Select the month you want to export.
3. Tap **Export as PDF**.
4. Share or save the PDF.

## What the PDF includes

- Monthly income and expense summary
- Category breakdown with amounts
- Account balances
- Budget performance
- Top merchants

## Use cases

- Share a monthly summary with a partner or family member.
- Keep a paper-trail record of monthly finances.
- Send to an accountant.
