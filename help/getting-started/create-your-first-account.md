# Create your first account

Rasmerk uses **accounts** to track where your money is. Add one account for each real-world wallet, bank, or card you want to track.

## Steps

1. Open the app and tap **Accounts** in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap the **+** button.
3. Choose an account type:
   - **Cash** — physical cash in hand
   - **Bank** — savings or current bank account
   - **Wallet** — digital wallets (Google Pay, Paytm, etc.)
   - **Credit Card** — card balance (liability)
4. Enter a name, currency, and optional opening balance.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Free tier limit

The free plan allows up to **5 accounts**. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited accounts.

> **Tip:** Set an accurate Opening Balance so your account totals reflect your real balance from day one. Opening Balance is not counted as income.
