What is Rasmerk?

Rasmerk is an offline-first personal money manager for Android. It tracks your income, expenses, and transfers across multiple accounts â€” without requiring internet or a cloud account.

Key principles

  • Your data stays on your phone. All transactions are stored in a local SQLite database.
  • No account required. You can use Rasmerk without signing in to any service.
  • No cloud database. Rasmerk servers never receive your financial data.
  • Optional cloud backup. Google Drive backup is available (Premium) â€” encrypted before upload.

What Rasmerk tracks

  • Cash, bank accounts, wallets, and credit cards
  • Income, expenses, and transfers between accounts
  • Budgets by category
  • Money owed to and from friends (IOUs and loans)
  • Recurring and pending transactions
