# Opening Balance explained

When you create an account, you can set an **Opening Balance** — the amount already in that account before you started tracking.

## Important: Opening Balance is NOT income

Opening Balance is a starting point, not a transaction. It does not appear in your income totals or monthly reports.

## Example

You have ₹10,000 in your savings account before installing Rasmerk. Set Opening Balance = ₹10,000. Your account shows the correct total without distorting your income report.

## When to use it

- You are migrating from another app and want correct balances.
- You are starting mid-month and already have money in accounts.

## Editing Opening Balance

Go to **Accounts → tap the account → Edit → Opening Balance**.
