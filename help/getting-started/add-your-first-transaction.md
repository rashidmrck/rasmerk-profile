# Add your first transaction

## Steps

1. Tap the **+** button (floating action button) on any screen.
2. Choose transaction type:
   - **Expense** — money going out
   - **Income** — money coming in
   - **Transfer** — moving money between your accounts
3. Enter the amount, category, date, and merchant/note.
4. Select the account.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Transaction types explained

| Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| Expense | Deducts from account balance, counts in reports |
| Income | Adds to account balance, counts in reports |
| Transfer | Moves balance between accounts, does NOT count as income or expense |
| Opening Balance | Sets account starting point — not counted as income |
