# I owe a friend — Track repayments

When a friend lends you money, record it so Rasmerk shows you exactly what you owe and tracks each repayment.

## Steps

1. Go to **Friends** in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap the friend's name (or add a new friend).
3. Tap **+ Add IOU / Loan**.
4. Choose direction: **I owe them**.
5. Enter the amount, date, and optional due date.
6. Tap **Save**.

## Recording a repayment

1. Open the loan from the Friends screen.
2. Tap **Add Repayment**.
3. Enter the repayment amount, date, and account used.
4. Tap **Save**.

Rasmerk updates the outstanding balance automatically.

## Immutability rule

Once the first repayment is recorded, the **original loan amount and direction cannot be changed**. Only the title, note, and due date remain editable.
