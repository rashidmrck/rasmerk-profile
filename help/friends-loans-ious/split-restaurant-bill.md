# Split a restaurant bill

Use the **Bill Split** wizard to divide a restaurant or any shared expense between friends.

## Steps

1. Scan the receipt or enter the total amount.
2. Tap **Split Bill**.
3. Add each person (friends from your list or new names).
4. Assign items or amounts to each person.
5. Rasmerk calculates what each person owes you.
6. Confirm — each amount is saved as an IOU against the relevant friend.

## Split methods

- **Equal split** — total divided equally among all members.
- **Custom amounts** — assign different amounts to each person.
- **Item-based** — assign specific receipt items to specific people.

## After splitting

Each friend's balance in Rasmerk is updated with their share. Collect payment and record repayments as they pay you back.
