Friend owes me money — Record an IOU

When you lend money to a friend, record it so Rasmerk tracks the outstanding balance.

Steps

  1. Go to Friends in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the friend's name (or add a new friend).
  3. Tap + Add IOU / Loan.
  4. Choose direction: They owe me.
  5. Enter the amount, date, and optional note.
  6. Select the account the money came from.
  7. Tap Save.

What happens

  • Rasmerk records a loan linked to that friend.
  • The amount is deducted from your selected account.
  • The Friends overview shows a running balance of what they owe you.

Free plan limit

Up to 3 active loans on the free plan. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited.

Editing a loan

You can edit the title, note, and due date at any time. Once any repayment is recorded, the amount and direction cannot be changed.
