# Category budgets

Each budget in Rasmerk is tied to a **specific category** (and optionally a sub-category).

## Examples

- Food → ₹8,000/month
- Food › Dining Out → ₹3,000/month
- Transport → ₹2,500/month
- Entertainment → ₹1,500/month

## Creating category budgets

1. Go to **Budgets → + New Budget**.
2. Tap the category field and select your category.
3. (Optional) Select a sub-category for more granular tracking.
4. Enter the monthly amount.
5. Tap Save.

## Free plan limit

Up to **3 category budgets** on the free plan. Premium = unlimited.

## How spending is counted

Expenses tagged with the matching category are automatically counted against the budget. Transfers and income are never counted.
