# Create a monthly budget

Budgets track how much you spend in a category each month and alert you when you are approaching the limit.

## Steps

1. Go to **Budgets** in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap **+ New Budget**.
3. Choose a **category** (e.g., Food, Transport, Entertainment).
4. Optionally choose a **sub-category**.
5. Enter the **monthly limit** amount.
6. Tap **Save**.

## Free plan limit

The free plan allows up to **3 budgets**. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited budgets.

## Budget alerts

Rasmerk sends a notification when you reach **80%** and **100%** of any budget. Each alert fires at most once per threshold per month.

## Viewing progress

The Budget screen shows:
- Amount spent vs. limit (progress bar)
- Remaining amount
- Safe to Spend per day
- Projected end-of-month spend (based on current burn rate)
