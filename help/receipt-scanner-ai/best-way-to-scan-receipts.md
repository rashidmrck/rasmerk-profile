# Best way to scan receipts

## Camera tips

1. **Lay the receipt flat** on a solid-colour surface (not on a patterned tablecloth).
2. **Use good lighting** — avoid harsh shadows or glare from overhead lights.
3. **Hold steady** or place the phone directly above the receipt.
4. **Fill the frame** — the receipt should take up most of the image.
5. **Capture the full receipt** including the top and bottom edges.

## What helps accuracy

- High contrast between text and paper (dark ink on white paper).
- Receipt is not crumpled or folded.
- Font is standard print — handwritten or dot-matrix receipts reduce accuracy.

## If items are missing

- Try **Improve with AI** mode for better parsing of complex receipts.
- Manually add missing items after scanning.

## Free limit

OCR scans are limited to **3 per month** on the free plan. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited scans.
