# Ads still showing after upgrade

## Only paid Premium removes ads

Ads in Rasmerk are only removed by a **paid in-app purchase (IAP)**. This is by design.

**Ambassador Premium** (earned through referrals) and **Referral time-based premium** unlock Premium features and remove quantity limits — but they **do not remove ads**.

## If you purchased IAP and still see ads

1. Go to **Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases**.
2. Ensure you are signed in with the same Google Play account used for the purchase.
3. Force-close and reopen the app.

## Confirming your premium type

Go to **Settings → Premium**. It shows:
- **Premium Active** (paid IAP) → ads removed
- **Ambassador Active** (referral) → ads remain
- **Free** → ads shown
