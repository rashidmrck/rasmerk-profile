# Premium not activated

## After purchase on the same device

If you just purchased and Premium is not showing:
1. Go to **Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases**.
2. Wait a few seconds for the Play Store to confirm the purchase.
3. Restart the app.

## After reinstalling or switching device

1. Sign in with the same **Google Play account** used for the purchase.
2. Go to **Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases**.

## Ambassador / Referral premium not showing

Ambassador premium syncs from the server. The app checks every 15 minutes (or on cold launch).

1. Force-close and reopen Rasmerk.
2. Check internet connection.
3. If your device clock is incorrect, referral premium may be suspended. Correct the clock and reopen.

## If nothing works

Contact support from **Settings → Help → Contact Us** with your Google Play order number.
