# Create multiple accounts

Rasmerk lets you track all your money in one place by creating separate accounts for each wallet, bank, or card.

## Steps

1. Go to **Accounts** in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap **+ Add Account**.
3. Choose an account type: Cash, Bank, Wallet, or Credit Card.
4. Enter a name, currency, and opening balance.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Built-in account types

- **Cash** — physical notes and coins
- **Bank** — savings or current account
- **Wallet** — digital wallets (UPI, Paytm, etc.)
- **Credit Card** — credit card balance (shown as liability)

## Custom account types (Premium)

Create your own types (e.g., "PPF Account", "Fixed Deposit"). Free plan allows 2 custom types; Premium allows unlimited.

## Free plan limit

Up to **5 accounts** on the free plan. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited.
