# Is an account required?

**No.** You do not need to create an account or sign in to use Rasmerk.

All features — transactions, budgets, accounts, friends — work without signing in.

## When sign-in is needed

A Google account is required only for:
- **Google Drive Backup** (Premium)
- **Ambassador Program** (referral rewards)

For everything else, Rasmerk works completely locally with no account.
