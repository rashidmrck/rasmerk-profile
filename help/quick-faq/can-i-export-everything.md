# Can I export everything?

**Yes.** Rasmerk lets you export all your financial data.

## Export options

| Format | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| CSV / TSV (all transactions) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Google Drive Backup (.mmbk) | ❌ | ✅ |
| PDF monthly report | ❌ | ✅ |

## CSV export includes

- All transactions with date, account, category, amount, and notes
- All transaction types: income, expense, transfer, opening balance

## How to export

Go to **Settings → Export → CSV** to download your data at any time.
