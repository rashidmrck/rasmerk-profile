# Is my data on Rasmerk servers?

**No.** Rasmerk does not have a cloud database for financial data.

All your transactions, accounts, budgets, and financial records are stored in a **local SQLite database on your device**.

Rasmerk's servers only store:
- Anonymous referral tracking data (Firebase UID, referral records)
- Anonymous usage analytics

Your financial data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable Google Drive Backup (Premium).
