# Hide Balance mode

Hide Balance mode replaces all visible amounts with `••••••` so no one nearby can see your finances.

## How to toggle

Tap the **eye icon** in the top bar on the Dashboard or Accounts screen.

## What gets hidden

- Dashboard: total balance, monthly income/expense, safe-to-spend
- Account list: all account balances and net worth
- Budget cards: spent amounts and remaining amounts
- Transaction list: all transaction amounts
- Friends card: amounts owed to/from friends

## Persistent setting

You can also enable **Always hide balances on launch** from **Settings → Security → Hide Balances**. When enabled, every app launch starts with amounts hidden.

When App Lock is enabled, Hide Balance is also automatically active.
