# Free vs Premium limits

## Quantity limits

| Feature | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts | 5 | Unlimited |
| Categories | 10 | Unlimited |
| Budgets | 3 | Unlimited |
| Loans / IOUs | 3 | Unlimited |
| Pending transactions | 3 | Unlimited |
| Custom account types | 2 | Unlimited |
| CSV/XLS imports | 2 (lifetime) | Unlimited |
| SMS auto-import | 20/month | Unlimited |
| OCR receipt scans | 3/month | Unlimited |

## Premium-only features

| Feature | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-currency | ❌ | ✅ |
| Google Drive Backup | ❌ | ✅ |
| PDF Reports | ❌ | ✅ |
| Background SMS sync | ❌ | ✅ |
| Advanced SMS filters | ❌ | ✅ |
| Learned SMS rules | ❌ | ✅ |
| Smart merchant aliases | ❌ | ✅ |

## Ads

Ads are only removed with a **paid IAP Premium** purchase. Referral/Ambassador premium does not remove ads.

## How to upgrade

Go to **Settings → Premium** to view upgrade options.
