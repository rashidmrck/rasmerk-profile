# What is the Ambassador Program?

The Ambassador Program lets you **earn Premium access by referring friends** to Rasmerk — no payment required.

## How it works

1. Get your unique referral link from **Settings → Ambassador**.
2. Share it with friends: `rasmerk.com/r/RM[code]`
3. Your friend installs Rasmerk, opens your link, and uses the app.
4. When they activate (add an account + first transaction), you earn Premium days.

## Rewards

| Milestone | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1st successful referral | 7 days Premium |
| 3rd successful referral | 30 days Premium |
| 5th+ referral | Ambassador Unlimited (removes quantity limits) |

## Note on ads

Ambassador Premium **does not remove ads**. Only a paid IAP purchase removes ads.

## Annual limit

A maximum of **10 referral rewards** per calendar year (UTC).
