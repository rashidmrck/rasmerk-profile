# Premium days earned

When a referral activates, Rasmerk credits Premium days to your account.

## How activation works

1. Your friend meets all activation criteria (account + transaction added).
2. Rasmerk's server validates the referral (5-minute anti-abuse delay).
3. Premium days are added to your entitlement on the server.
4. The app syncs on your next cold launch or within 15 minutes.
5. Your Premium status activates.

## Viewing your Premium status

Go to **Settings → Premium** to see:
- Premium type (IAP / Referral / Ambassador Unlimited)
- Expiry date (for time-limited referral premium)

## Clock tamper protection

Referral Premium is validated server-side. If Rasmerk detects the device clock has been rolled back or jumped forward by more than 30 days, referral Premium is suspended until the next successful server sync.

## Referral premium does not remove ads

Ads are only removed by paid IAP Premium.
