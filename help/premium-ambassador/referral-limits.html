Referral limits

Annual cap

Rasmerk allows a maximum of 10 rewarded referrals per calendar year (January 1 – December 31, UTC).

After 10 rewarded referrals, additional referrals in that calendar year are tracked but do not grant additional rewards until the new year.

Per-referral cooldown

Each referral requires the referred friend to wait at least 5 minutes after account creation before activation is counted. This prevents instant fake activations.

Other limits

  • Each person can only be referred once — a friend who already has Rasmerk cannot count as a new referral.
  • Self-referral is not possible.
