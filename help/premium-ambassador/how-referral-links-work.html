What happens when a friend opens your link

  1. Friend taps the link â†’ opens Rasmerk (or prompts to install from Play Store).
  2. The referral code is captured automatically.
  3. Friend uses the app and adds at least 1 account and 1 transaction.
  4. After 5 minutes, the referral is validated by Rasmerk's server.
  5. Your account is credited with the reward days on the next sync.

Activation requirements (friend must meet all)

  • Installed the app via your link
  • Signed in with a Google account
  • Added at least 1 account
  • Added at least 1 transaction
  • Waited at least 5 minutes (anti-abuse cooldown)

When you receive the reward

Rewards are synced from the server. The app checks entitlement every 15 minutes (or on cold launch). Premium days activate immediately after sync.
