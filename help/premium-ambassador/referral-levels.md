# Referral levels

Rasmerk rewards grow as you refer more friends.

## Reward milestones

| Referrals activated | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1st referral | **7 days Premium** |
| 3rd referral | **30 days Premium** |
| 5th referral | **Ambassador Unlimited** — removes all quantity limits permanently (while active) |

## How rewards stack

Each milestone reward is separate. Reaching the 3rd referral gives 30 bonus days on top of any existing premium time.

## Ambassador Unlimited

At 5 referrals, you unlock **Ambassador Unlimited**, which removes the free-plan quantity caps:
- Unlimited accounts (was 5)
- Unlimited categories (was 10)
- Unlimited budgets (was 3)
- Unlimited loans (was 3)
- Unlimited CSV imports

**Note:** Ambassador Unlimited does NOT remove ads. Only paid IAP removes ads.
