Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Rasmerk ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Money Manager mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

1. Data Collection

Money Manager is designed as an offline-first, privacy-first application. We collect no personal data on our servers. All data you enter or import (transactions, accounts, categories, budgets) is stored exclusively on your own device in a local SQLite database.

2. Data You Provide

  • Transaction data (amounts, merchants, dates, categories)
  • Account names and aliases
  • Budget limits and categories
  • Friends and loan records

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Export feature to share a file.

3. SMS Access

On Android, the App requests permission to read your SMS inbox to automatically detect and import bank and UPI transaction messages. This processing happens entirely on your device. No SMS content is transmitted to any external server.

4. Gmail Access

If you choose to use the Gmail Import feature, you will be asked to sign in with Google. The App uses the Gmail API to read emails matching financial transaction patterns. These emails are processed on-device. We do not store, transmit, or retain any Gmail content on our servers.

5. Camera & Photos

The Screenshot OCR feature uses your device camera or photo gallery to extract transaction information using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. No images are uploaded to any server.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party SDKs which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In / Gmail API (Google LLC)
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition (Google LLC)

7. Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device. You can enable PIN or biometric protection via the App Lock feature. We recommend enabling this feature and keeping your device secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do so.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@rasmerk.com.
