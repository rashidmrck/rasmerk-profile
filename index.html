Our Philosophy

Built for privacy,
built for you.

Rasmerk is an independent software brand focused on building tools that solve real, everyday problems — starting with personal finance.

Money Manager was born from a simple frustration: existing personal finance apps were either too complex, locked behind cloud accounts, or failed to automate transaction logging. So we built a privacy-first, fully local alternative.

Every feature is designed to put you in control. The goal is software that remains fast, private, and offline-first — getting out of your way while quietly organizing your financial life.
