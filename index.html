Our Philosophy

Built for privacy,
built for you.

Rasmerk is an independent software brand focused on building tools that solve real, everyday problems — starting with personal finance.

Money Manager was born from a simple frustration: existing personal finance apps were either too complex, locked behind cloud accounts, or failed to automate transaction logging. Core tracking, SMS imports, and standard receipt scanning work on your device.

Optional features use online services. Google Drive is used for Drive backups, and Gemini processes bills when you choose Cloud AI Split. Read our privacy policy.
